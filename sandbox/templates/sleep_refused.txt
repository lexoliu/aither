This command runs `sleep` in the foreground, which only serves polling — the call was refused. To wait on a background task, do nothing: its completion is delivered to you when it finishes. To follow a command's or a file's output as it arrives, use `monitor`. To wait on a condition, run a background script (`background: true`) that exits when the condition holds, e.g. `until [ -f done ]; do sleep 5; done`. For a one-off look at a task's output, use `terminal_read`.
